Updates and the TV

Update the Hub and the box from here. The box also updates itself every Monday at 4 am.

Hub …

    To update the Hub from the phone, run this once on the server: sudo bash /containers/UltimateTV/tools/hub/update.sh --phone-updates

    Box …

    After each update the box checks that the app starts, reaches the Hub, plays a test clip and still sees the TV remote's adapter. If not, it rolls the update back by itself and tells you here and on the TV.

    What's new

    What has changed in the app, newest first. The TV and the phone remote show the new things once after an update.

    Hub backup …

    Every night the Hub copies its settings, profiles, logins, series links and watch history to the NAS.

      Theme

      The look of the TV, this remote and these pages. Tap one to try it: the TV changes straight away too.

      App name

      What the app is called on the TV, the phone remote, these pages and in the Spotify app. Leave it blank to go back to UltimateTV.

      Picture and sound

      The box switches the TV to each film's frame rate and sends HDR, and passes Dolby Digital Plus (with Atmos) to the TV's speakers.

      Screensaver

      When the TV's left with nothing playing, the screen dims and later a screensaver starts. Any button on the remote brings it back without doing anything else. Paused video counts as nothing playing.

      Weather Not set

      Today's weather on the TV's Home, the temperature along the top and on the screensaver, and the week ahead (on the phone remote's Watch tab too). From Open-Meteo: free, no account.

      Recording

      Music channels …

      Live channels the Hub makes from music videos it downloads. Each pass through a channel is a fresh shuffle.

      Profiles and PIN

      Kids profiles only see kids' channels and titles rated up to their limit. Set a PIN on the adult profiles to stop anyone switching out of Kids, or changing settings, without it.

      Phones and remotes

      Only phones and screens paired with the TV can work it. To add a phone, scan the code in Settings › Phones and remotes on the TV. Remove anything you don't recognise. Features under each screen switch parts of the app off on that screen only.

        Home Assistant and scripts

        Pop-ups, news flashes, key presses and the media player controls (play, pause, stop, volume, channels, standby) from Home Assistant or a script need this key, sent as the header X-Api-Key (or Authorization: Bearer …). It can't change settings. docs/notifications.md has the Home Assistant set-up.

        Told when a film or programme starts, pauses, resumes, stops, reaches the credits, the channel changes or someone switches profile, so Home Assistant can dim the lights. Leave empty to send nothing.

        Android apps

        Tap to download on this phone, or let another phone scan the code. Open the download to install it (allow installs from Chrome if Android asks). Works on the home Wi-Fi only; the app pairs itself with the TV when it's first opened.

                Films and series sources

                TMDB gives artwork and discovery rails. AIOStreams finds streams, and TorBox plays and downloads them.

                  Scores and subtitles

                  OMDb adds IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic scores to each film's page (a free key from omdbapi.com). OpenSubtitles finds subtitles a film doesn't come with: make a free account at opensubtitles.com, then add an API consumer under your profile for the key. Without the login it's 5 downloads a day; with it, 20.

                  Where things are saved

                  Folders as the home server sees them. Use the same ones Jellyfin scans so recordings and downloads show up there.
