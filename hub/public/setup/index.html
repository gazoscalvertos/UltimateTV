Updates and the TV

Update the Hub and the box from here. The box also updates itself every Monday at 4 am.

Hub …

    To update the Hub from the phone, run this once on the server: sudo bash /containers/UltimateTV/tools/hub/update.sh --phone-updates

    Box …

    App name

    What the app is called on the TV, the phone remote, these pages and in the Spotify app. Leave it blank to go back to UltimateTV.

    Picture and sound

    The box switches the TV to each film's frame rate and sends HDR, and passes Dolby Digital Plus (with Atmos) to the TV's speakers.

    Screensaver

    When the TV's left with nothing playing, the screen dims and later a screensaver starts. Any button on the remote brings it back without doing anything else. Paused video counts as nothing playing.

    Recording

    Music channels …

    Live channels the Hub makes from music videos it downloads. Each pass through a channel is a fresh shuffle.

    Profiles and PIN

    Kids profiles only see kids' channels and titles rated up to their limit. Set a PIN on the adult profiles to stop anyone switching out of Kids, or changing settings, without it.

    Phones and remotes

    Only phones and screens paired with the TV can work it. To add a phone, scan the code in Settings › Phones and remotes on the TV. Remove anything you don't recognise.

      Home Assistant and scripts

      Pop-ups, news flashes and key presses from Home Assistant or a script need this key, sent as the header X-Api-Key (or Authorization: Bearer …). It can't change settings or play things.

      Live TV Not set up

      Your IPTV provider's details. They stay on the home server and never reach the TV.

        TV guide

        Guide lists and channels

        The lists along the top of the TV Guide and their order, which list each profile's guide opens on, your custom guides, and every channel's number, category and favourite. The TV shows changes straight away.
