Tips

  1. Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Windows, Mac and Linux. Phones can't share their screen from a browser: use AirPlay on an iPhone, or the phone remote's Cast tab to send a photo, video or link.
  2. The first time, the browser warns that this page's certificate isn't trusted. It's the Hub's own: choose Advanced, then continue to the page.
  3. Second screen (extend): on Windows, install a virtual display driver (such as the free "Virtual Display Driver") and share the new screen; on a Mac, use AirPlay instead (Control Centre › Screen Mirroring › the TV › Use as separate display).
  4. Fine for slides, photos, web pages and video calls. For games, use Moonlight from the TV's Cast and games screen: it's much quicker.
